# AIRP_PRE_BETA_HYPOTHESIS

Status: FROZEN PRE-BETA HYPOTHESIS
Date: 2026-09-18

## H1 — AiRP x FlowSignal complementarity
For a subset of AI Risk Profiler Feature Blocks where a consequential action depends on authority that can change between approval and execution, FlowSignal may provide an independently testable runtime control and evidence mechanism relevant to AiRP Step 5, with resulting evidence potentially informing Step 6.

## Falsification conditions
H1 is false or materially narrowed if:
1. AiRP already establishes equivalent independent execution-time authority control.
2. Applicable AiRP controls do not require or recognise this class of runtime safeguard.
3. FlowSignal artefacts cannot satisfy relevant evidential requirements.
4. Runtime Authority does not materially affect the relevant residual control gap.
5. The proposed mapping depends on changing FlowSignal's core decision semantics merely to fit AiRP.

## Explicit non-claims
FlowSignal does not by itself establish model accuracy, hallucination prevention, bias/fairness, clinical efficacy, model-drift detection, regulatory approval, product assurance generally, or an AiRP Step 6 finding.

An action can be authorised and still be clinically wrong.

## Experimental mapping rule
AiRP terminology remains an external assurance context. It must not be hard-coded into the core Runtime Authority decision engine. Any Step 5 mapping is CANDIDATE/EXPERIMENTAL until assessed under ORCHA's methodology. Step 6 remains NOT DETERMINED unless ORCHA determines it.
